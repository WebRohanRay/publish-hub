# Editorial Content Quality & Evaluation Rubric

Atlas Journal maintains uncompromising standards for investigative publishing. Every published piece undergoes rigorous verification before publication.

## Evaluation Scorecard (100 Points Total)

| Criterion | Max Points | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Investigation & Data** | 25 | Concrete metrics, packet analysis, financial audits, or direct testing rather than speculative summaries. |
| **Direct Answer AEO Clarity** | 20 | Immediate, unambiguous answer to core search query in an executive callout block. |
| **Comparative Visual Matrices** | 15 | Structured comparison table with benchmarked attributes. |
| **Information Gain & Novelty** | 15 | Insight that cannot be found on generic top-10 aggregator sites. |
| **On-Page Technical SEO** | 15 | Proper H1/H2/H3 hierarchy, schema markup, descriptive alt text, optimized slug. |
| **Editorial Tone & Grammar** | 10 | Authoritative, objective, engaging journalism without fluff or low-value filler. |

## Content Verification Workflow
1. Author drafts article with primary benchmark data.
2. Desk verifies screenshot evidence and empirical claims.
3. Editorial team confirms source attribution and legal compliance.
4. Final approval moves article from `draft` to `published` in Supabase.
