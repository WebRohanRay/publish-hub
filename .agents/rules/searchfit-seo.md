# SearchFIT SEO & AEO Operating Directives

When the user asks about their blog, article content, or SEO/AEO optimization:

## 1. Tool Activation
- Always use the **SearchFit SEO plugin / MCP server** if it is available and connected.

## 2. Mandatory 8-Step SEO/AEO Execution Workflow
Whenever analyzing, writing, or optimizing a blog post, always execute this full 8-step blueprint:

1. **Audit the Post**:
   - Perform a comprehensive technical, semantic, and editorial audit of the post.
   - Identify weak hooks, content gaps, heading structure issues, readability bottlenecks, and competitive blind spots.

2. **Current SERP & AI Overview Research**:
   - Research current top-ranking organic competitors and AI-Overview-cited sources (Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT citations) for the topic.
   - Benchmark the consensus answers vs. unique insider angles.

3. **Keyword Strategy (Search Intent Grounded)**:
   - Suggest 1 high-intent Primary Keyword.
   - Suggest 4 to 6 semantically related secondary/LSI keywords based on real search intent (informational, transactional, investigative).

4. **Substantive Rewrite & Expansion**:
   - Rewrite and expand the content properly with data-backed benchmarks, structural clarity, and engaging narrative flow.
   - Strictly avoid low-value filler, generic fluff, or padding just to hit an arbitrary word count.

5. **Complete On-Page SEO Checklist**:
   - Title tag (50–60 characters, high CTR hook)
   - Meta description (145–160 characters with clear call-to-action)
   - URL-safe clean slug
   - Internal links (natural contextual bridges to related topic clusters)
   - External links to authoritative benchmark sources
   - Heading structure (single H1, clean H2/H3 distribution)

6. **Visual Asset Strategy & Alt Text**:
   - Suggest relevant images, comparison tables, or infographics.
   - Provide descriptive, keyword-rich alt text for every asset.

7. **Realistic Backlink & Promotion Ideas**:
   - Provide concrete, non-spam promotion angles (digital PR hooks, community seeding, niche forum citation, resource page outreach).

8. **Post-Publish Telemetry & Analytics Tracking**:
   - Specify the exact Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Search Console (GSC) metrics to monitor (e.g., query impressions, CTR, average SERP position, engagement duration, scroll depth milestones).
